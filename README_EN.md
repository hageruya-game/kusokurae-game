# Pressure Corridor (圧力回廊)

**"Don't obey."** — A peer pressure survival game in your mobile browser.

You will be ordered, taunted, and pressured.
Can you keep thinking for yourself?
It's short — but it stays with you.

## How to Play

- Open on your mobile browser (no install needed)
- Tap and swipe to play
- 3 stages, 10–15 minutes to complete

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 — Orders | Obey or resist. Find the pattern |
| Stage 2 — Slash | Swipe to cut through commands. Speed and judgment |
| Stage 3 — Crowd | They all look the same. Find the one that's different |
